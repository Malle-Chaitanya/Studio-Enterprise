# HR Policies — Neutara Technologies

## Policy 1: Shift Allowances

### Night Shift Allowance
- Office-only — WFH does not qualify
- Must be on the approved shift roster (9pm–6am IST or 7pm–4am IST)
- Full shift must be completed
- Requires: Biometric + Zoho + Insightful attendance records
- Not applicable for unapproved/ad-hoc work or missing attendance

### Comp-Off (Compensatory Off)
- Earned by working on weekends or declared holidays
- Must be pre-approved by Team Lead on Zoho before working
- Must be used within the same calendar year — lapses if unused, no carryforward

### Comp-Off Allowance (Monetary)
- Exceptional cases only — needs Team Lead + HR approval
- Minimum 7.5 working hours required to qualify
- Calculated strictly on Insightful data

### Holiday + Night Shift Same Day
- Only Comp-Off applies — night shift allowance does NOT apply
- Dual benefits (night shift + comp-off allowance) are NOT allowed on the same day

Compliance: All approvals require Team Lead + HR verification. HR decision is final.

## Policy 2: Employee Referral Policy

Effective: 1 February 2026

Who can refer: All permanent full-time employees EXCEPT HR department members and the hiring panel for that specific role.

### Referral Process
- Email referral to HR — candidate's resume must include your name and department
- Candidate completes all standard rounds (technical, HR, managerial)
- Selection is purely merit-based — referral does not guarantee hiring
- Referrer must not attempt to influence the process

### Bonus Structure
| Level | Experience | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Junior | 1–3 years | ₹3,000 |
| Mid-Level | 4–10 years | ₹5,000 |
| Senior | 10+ years | ₹10,000 |

Payment: After referred employee completes 3 continuous months. Processed in next payroll cycle. No bonus if they leave before 3 months.

Exclusions: Rejected candidates blocked 6 months. Already-applied candidates don't qualify. Ex-employees/interns/contractors need HR approval. Late referrals (after candidate applied) are invalid.

Disqualification: Influencing the process, falsified info, undisclosed family/conflict of interest.

## Policy 3: Loan Policy

Eligibility: 1 year continuous service. Interest-free. Subject to fund availability. Genuine verifiable purpose required. Employee pays any applicable tax.

Amount: Max = 3 months gross salary. Higher needs COO/CEO approval.

Repayment: 12 equal monthly salary deductions starting the month after sanction. Early full repayment via cheque allowed — inform HR in writing. Outstanding loan must be cleared before exit/final settlement.

### Application Process
- Send formal email request to HR
- HR verifies eligibility → forwards to CEO
- CEO approves or rejects (sole discretion)
- Finance processes payment on approval

Cooling Period: Must wait 12 months after repaying before applying again.

## Policy 4: Employee Conduct & Etiquette Policy

### Professional Behaviour & Decorum
- Conduct yourself with professionalism, integrity, and respect at all times
- Maintain positive, courteous, and collaborative attitude with colleagues, clients, stakeholders
- Avoid behaviour that disrupts workplace or affects others' performance
- Dress appropriately and maintain basic hygiene per workplace norms

### Punctuality & Work Discipline
- Adhere to assigned work timings, schedules, and shift requirements
- Be attentive, engaged, and available during work hours, meetings, and discussions
- Follow all attendance, leave request, and reporting protocols
- Use office resources, infrastructure, and company assets responsibly

### Substance-Free Workplace
- Alcohol, drugs, or intoxicating substances during office hours or on premises is strictly prohibited
- Any violation may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination

### Workplace Relationships & Boundaries
- Personal relationships leading to favouritism, conflicts of interest, or discomfort are not acceptable
- Maintain professional boundaries at all times; hostile or inappropriate behaviour will not be tolerated

### Respectful Communication
- All communication (verbal, written, digital) must be respectful, clear, and professional
- Zero tolerance for abusive language, harassment, bullying, discrimination, or derogatory remarks

### Confidentiality & Data Handling
- Safeguard client, company, and team information strictly
- Do not share or expose sensitive information to unauthorized individuals
- Follow all security protocols to avoid data breaches or misuse

### Use of Technology & Devices
- Use company systems and devices only for authorized work purposes
- Personal use should be minimal, non-disruptive, and must not affect productivity

### Conduct During Meetings & Calls
- Join meetings on time; minimize distractions; stay fully present
- Respond to invitations promptly; if declining, give reason and suggest alternative time

### Workplace Safety & Cleanliness
- Maintain a clean, hygienic, and organized workspace
- Follow all safety guidelines and report hazards immediately

Zero Tolerance for Misconduct: Substance use, harassment, discrimination, confidentiality breaches, violence, threats, wilful misconduct, insubordination, or repeated violations.

## Policy 5: Leave Policy

### Annual Leave Entitlement
- Total 24 leaves per year: 12 Paid/Casual Leaves (CL) + 12 Sick Leaves (SL)
- Credited monthly at 1 CL + 1 SL per month

### Paid/Casual Leave (CL)
- 1 CL credited per month
- Must apply 7 days in advance via email to reporting manager (looping HR) + Zoho People

### Sick Leave (SL)
- 1 SL credited per month
- Can be availed on short notice or same day for medical/emergency situations
- Must be regularized in Zoho People within 2 days of resuming duty

### Maternity Leave (ML)
- Eligible: female employees with minimum 1 year of service
- 6 months paid ML (per Maternity Benefit Act 2016)
- Can start earliest 10 weeks before expected delivery date
- Additional 2 months unpaid ML available for medical emergency
- ML period cannot be included in hike calculation period

### Paternity Leave (PL)
- Eligible: male employees with minimum 3 months of service
- Maximum 5 days paid paternity leave
- Must be applied as pre-planned leave

### Compensatory Off
- Eligible when working on weekend/national/festival/declared off day
- Must be availed within the same calendar year (lapses on 31st Dec)

### Carryforward Rules
- Max 6 CL can be carried forward to next year, to be used by 1st June
- Remaining balance and all SL lapse at year end

### Leave Without Approval
- Absence without proper approval = LOP (Loss of Pay) + disciplinary action

## Policy 6: Exit & FnF Policy

### Notice Period
- Standard notice period: 90 days
- During notice period, employee must provide Knowledge Transfer (KT) to replacement
- No unpaid/non-paid leave allowed during notice period
- Any leave during NP leads to either extension of NP days or Loss of Pay

### Exit Formalities
- Employee must return all company assets: laptop, access cards, devices, and any company property
- Access termination is a mandatory part of the exit process

### Clearance Procedures / Asset Submission
- Complete all clearance procedures before last working day
- Return company property and settle any outstanding dues or expenses
- Employees on garden leave must submit assets immediately

### Garden Leave
- In some cases, employer may ask employee not to report during notice period
- Employee is still considered employed and receives full salary and benefits during garden leave

### Termination Process
- Employment may be terminated due to: poor performance, policy violations, data breach, or client data escalations
- In termination cases, employee may be relieved immediately without serving notice period

### FnF (Full & Final) Settlement
- FnF processed within 60 days from last working day
- Employee receives: no dues clearance document + relieving documents
- Employee must sign the copy after 60 days to complete the FnF process
